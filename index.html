About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at The University of Texas at Dallas, where I hold a 3.83 graduate GPA and was elected to The Honor Society of Phi Kappa Phi in 2026. My background is in differential equations, numerical methods for PDEs and mathematical modeling of blood flow.

Before coming to UT Dallas, I spent nine years teaching mathematics as a Lecturer and then Assistant Professor at Bangladesh Army International University of Science & Technology. My current work moves from classical modeling toward topological data analysis and machine learning for medical image analysis.

Research

Brain tumor detection and segmentation in BraTS 2026 MRI

UT Dallas · supervised by Prof. Yan Cao · 2026 · MATLAB

  • Built an end-to-end pipeline for whole-tumor segmentation on T2-FLAIR slices from five BraTS 2026 cases.
  • Designed an adaptive threshold: robust normalization, Otsu thresholding with a 98th-percentile fallback, then morphological refinement.
  • Extended it to split enhancing from non-enhancing tumor using T1 contrast enhancement (mean Dice 0.70 and 0.72).
  • Found that lesion size, not method complexity, drives failure: the next step is shape-aware, topological features for small lesions.
0.807Mean Dice
0.715Mean IoU
0.843Mean recall
0.992Mean specificity

Dice reached 0.94–0.97 on the three large lesions.
